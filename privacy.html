Power Hand uses the camera to display a live preview, track the position and pose of one hand, and render visual effects. Hand tracking and image processing take place locally on the device. Camera frames and hand landmarks are temporary and are not recorded, uploaded, or retained by the application.

Data collection and sharing

The production application does not collect or share personal data, does not provide user accounts, does not display advertisements, does not use analytics, and does not connect to application servers. Its Android release does not request the Internet permission. Camera permission is required for the experience. Vibration permission supports optional tactile feedback.

Third-party components

MediaPipe provides the on-device hand-tracking model. Its Android SDK's default remote statistics logger is disabled in this application, so no tracking statistics are transmitted. Expo development and build services are used to develop and distribute the software; development builds are separate from the offline production application's runtime behavior.

Security and retention

Camera frames and derived hand landmarks exist only in memory while the camera is active and are discarded immediately as they are replaced. Because the application does not collect or store personal data, there is no stored user data to retain or delete, and there is no account to delete.

Your choices

You can revoke camera access at any time in the device's application settings. Without camera access, the live tracking experience is unavailable. The application does not store photos or recordings in a media library.

Children

Power Hand is offered to users aged 6 and older. The application does not collect, store, or share personal information from children or from any other user. Camera frames and hand landmarks are processed only on the device and are never transmitted. There is no advertising, no analytics, and no user account, so no personal data is held for a child and no separate deletion process is required. A parent or guardian with questions can contact us at the address below.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted at this address with an updated effective date. Material changes will apply only to future versions of the application.
